## Exchange 1

**Question:** I need to make one simple phone call, but I have reorganized my desk three times instead. How do I stop turning a five-minute task into a three-act tragedy?

**Mode:** Campfire Council Mode

**Analysis:** uncertainty + anger

### Output

Marcus Aurelius: Name the concern before it becomes your whole weather system with office furniture.
Ryan Holiday: This is information, not a prophecy; let it shrink back to normal human size.
Mel Robbins: Name the concern before it becomes your whole weather system with office furniture.
Marcus Aurelius: The obstacle is not the problem; your dramatic monologue about it might be.
Ryan Holiday: Make action physical: do one tiny visible action before the stress hires a marching band.
Mel Robbins: The council sees uncertainty; answer it with one small act, not a grand identity trial.

The Gavel Falls:

What they disagree on: Marcus Aurelius wants less grasping; Ryan Holiday wants cleaner judgment; Mel Robbins wants a bolder experiment.
Hidden pattern: You are not solving all of need make one simple; you are choosing the next honest move.
Tiny next action: Make one visible move on need make one simple before trying to solve the whole saga.
Ridiculous but useful reminder: Need Make One Simple does not get to rent the entire control room.
